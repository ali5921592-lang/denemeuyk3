# Sleepify Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 13 August 2026

## In short

Sleepify collects no personal data, sends nothing to any server and shares
nothing with third parties. The app has no network connection; all data stays
on your device.

## What is stored

The following is kept only in your device's local storage:

- Alarm times, repeat days and settings
- Sleep session records, durations and calculated scores
- Sound mixer preferences and language choice
- Any audio or video files you add
- Contact details you enter for the fake call feature

This data is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can clear it any time with
the "Delete sleep records" button.

## Camera

The camera is used only for the "dismiss alarm by scanning a barcode" task.
No image is recorded, stored or transmitted. Each frame is analysed for a
barcode and discarded immediately.

## Microphone

The microphone is used only when "night noise detection" is enabled.
**No audio is recorded.** The app measures the instantaneous sound level and
counts the times it crosses a threshold. There is no recording, speech
recognition or transmission.

## Motion sensor

The accelerometer is used only for the "shake to dismiss" task and the smart
wake window. Nothing is stored.

## Notifications

Notifications appear only when an alarm rings. Content is generated on device.

## Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code.

## Children

Because no data is collected, the app is suitable for all ages. "Baby mode" is
aimed at parents; keep the device away from the baby and at a low volume.

## Contact
